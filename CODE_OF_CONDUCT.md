# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This project exists to build good software together. Participation is open to anyone who
is willing to work constructively.

We expect everyone taking part — contributors, maintainers, reviewers and people opening
issues — to:

- be respectful and direct, and to critique code rather than people;
- assume good faith and ask for clarification before assuming bad intent;
- accept that technical disagreement is normal and is resolved with arguments and evidence;
- respect a maintainer's decision once a discussion has concluded;
- keep all project communication in English so that everyone can follow it.

We do not accept:

- harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks;
- discriminatory language or behaviour of any kind;
- publishing other people's private information;
- deliberately derailing discussions, or repeatedly reopening settled decisions;
- posting other people's credentials, customer data or confidential material.

## Scope

This applies to every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages,
code comments, and any public communication where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report a problem privately to the maintainer through
[GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/iamjonatha/decisionkit-dotnet/security/advisories/new)
or by contacting [@iamjonatha](https://github.com/iamjonatha) directly.

Reports are handled confidentially. Depending on severity, the response may be a private
warning, removal of the offending content, a temporary restriction, or a permanent ban
from the project.

## Attribution

The expectations above are aligned with the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), which you may consult for a
broader treatment of the subject.
